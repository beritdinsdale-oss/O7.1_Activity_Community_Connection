Find Your Community Connection

Climate-resilient gardening doesn’t stop at the garden gate. Follow something you care about outward and see who is already working on it with others.

🌳
🌼🌱

1. What draws your attention?

Choose one or two areas you’d be interested in connecting with beyond your own garden.

2. Where might people be working on this?

The work you care about may be happening under a name you wouldn’t expect.

Look beyond “climate” organizations.
Community gardens, habitat projects, watershed groups, libraries, parks programs, Extension programs, garden clubs, and informal networks may all be contributing to resilience.

3. Follow a thread into your community

There’s more than one way to find people doing this work. Choose a route that makes sense for you.

Start with what you already know

Think broadly: a community garden, garden club, restoration project, seed exchange, parks program, Extension program, or informal network can count. What matters is that people are working together on something connected to resilience.

Who could point you in the right direction?

Try another gardener, neighbor, librarian, Extension contact, conservation district staff member, or someone involved in a local garden or park.

A question you could ask:
“Do you know of people around here working on climate-resilient gardening?”
